Image Quality Candidates

An inventory of every 2D image in the game. For the images players see most, there are candidate replacements side by side with the current art, at the size a phone in portrait (1080 × 2340) shows them. Nothing here is in the game yet; this page is for choosing a direction.

The two directions are B, low-poly 3D scenes rendered in Blender, and C, flat vector art drawn by hand in code, in the shipped style but at the size the phone displays. The planned third direction, A (Meshy image generation), was not produced; see How these were made. Hub pictures are mock-ups: the art is framed the way Ui.Hero frames it (cover fit, rounded mask, scrim, rank, name and stars). The mock-up was checked against a device screenshot (below), so the hero's crop, size and text position match the phone.

Recommendation

The short version, per image family.

Schools → C

Hand-built vector, one building per school. All eight are distinct and each shows its name: a maple, a river, an evening gate, a shopfront. They are sharp at hub size and stay in the shipped style. B is a coherent set but looks like a toy diorama, and the buildings come out small.

Title + exam hall → B

The Blender classroom is the closest thing here to the approved reference's title screen: a real room with depth and evening light. The flat vector room (C) is fine but reads as a diagram.

Event objects → keep, sharpen

B is better on the document, the coins and the medal, about level on the envelope and note, and worse on the person. The cheaper win is drawing the current objects at display size (704 → 890 px wide).

Icons → fix five

The icon family is good apart from Coin, Hand, Question, Music and Gavel, which do not read at icon size. Redraws in the same stroke family are shown below.

Implementation note. The current art is generated from GameTheme.Current, so a theme swap restyles it (see Scenery.cs). If C is chosen, it should be ported into Scenery as drawing code at the hero's display size, not shipped as PNGs. The Python prototypes use the same primitives (boxes, triangles, discs, glows) plus polygon and ellipse fills that Painter would need. Baked PNGs (B, or C as images) would give up that rule. That would have to be a deliberate decision recorded in UI-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md. The candidates here are dark-theme only.

The eight schools (hub hero)

These follow M129's eight schools (branch m129-school-offers). The current column is what M129 shows: the building follows the school's kind, so four schools share the School scene and three share the colonnade. Each candidate is one picture per school. Select a picture to open the full art at 1600 × 1000.

Maple Elementary School · ★ · elementary

C. It is the only one where the name is visible before the title is read, and it is the friendliest first posting.

Riverside Middle School · ★★ · middle school

C. The bridge and reflections sit right at the top of the scrim, so they stay visible in the hero.

Eastgate Evening College · ★★ · adult evening and vocational

C, narrowly. B is close. Both say "evening" through a violet sky with no brass band. C's figures in the windows say "adults studying after work".

City High School · ★★★ · high school

C. It keeps the current clock-tower identity, so City High still looks like the school players know, just bigger, in the city, and sharper.

Brightpath Prep Centre · ★★ · private exam-prep centre

C. B is a strong second. Either one finally makes the prep centre look like the trade it is: a business, not a school.

Kingsley Academy · ★★★★ · selective academy

Tie; pick C for consistency. "Selective" comes across in both: walls, railings, a gate.

State University · ★★★★ · university

C. It is the current idea done properly, and the dome now separates it from the academy and the exam centre.

National Examination Centre · ★★★★★ · national examinations body

C. The desks inside tell the player what this building is for. It is the brightest picture of the eight, which fits the top rung. The atrium's glow stops above the rank line.

Event objects (career event cards)

The band across the top of a career event card is 890 × 253 and currently drawn at 704 × 200. Only direction B was made for these. A vector redraw would be a sharper version of the current objects, which is the cheaper option recommended above.

Icons

All 30 glyphs from Icons.cs at their native 96 px. The family holds together, but five do not read: Coin (a hash mark in a ring), Hand (reads as a comb), Question (the hook breaks apart), Music (two rings and a box) and Gavel (a slash and a square). Gear is borderline; it reads as a sun.

Not covered

  • Direction A (Meshy). Not produced; see below.
  • Shop items (detector, replay, lens, analyzer, shirt, badge): inventoried only. The current tiles are below.
  • App icon: inventoried only. It is a clear document-and-magnifier mark at 1024 px.
  • The other 25 icons were judged fine and not redrawn.
  • Light theme. Every candidate is dark-theme only. The current art follows the theme; a baked image would not.
  • On the phone. Nothing was installed; the phone is shared with other sessions. The hub frames are calibrated mock-ups.
  • Dead files. The nine unreferenced PNGs in Resources/UI could be deleted. Left alone, because this task changes no game files.

How these were made

  • Current art was rendered from the game's own code (Scenery, Props, Icons) inside the Unity editor, with the shipped dark theme and each school's M129 accent.
  • B: Blender. Scenes are built from primitives by script, with flat materials in the theme palette (navy ground, brass horizon, warm emissive windows). They are rendered in Eevee at 1600 × 1000 (schools), 1600 × 1300 (title) and 1408 × 400 (objects), about 2 seconds each. Each was built in its own temporary scene and removed afterwards.
  • C: Vector. Flat shapes drawn in Python at 3200 × 2000, downsampled to 1600 × 1000, with additive blurred glows. They use the same vocabulary as Scenery.cs, so they can be ported to it.
  • A: Meshy (not produced). One test image for Maple was generated with Meshy's text-to-image model (3 credits). It was not retrieved, and no further Meshy jobs were run in this session. Generation needs the owner's go-ahead. At 3 credits an image, three variants for each of the eight schools would cost about 72 of the 599 credits left.
  • Credits: 602 before, 599 after (3 spent). Game repository: no files changed and nothing committed.
