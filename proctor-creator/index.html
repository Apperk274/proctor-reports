Proctor Creator

The player's proctor is now the Quaternius-based character from the Proctor Character report, in the game: chosen at the start of every new career and changed any time in the Wardrobe's new You segment, for free. Body, face, brows, hair, facial hair, build, skin, hair and eye colours. Every Wardrobe item is worn on it, refitted by the art pipeline. All images are captures of the game (Unity, editor, a 1080 × 2340 phone screen). Students are unchanged.

Create your proctor

A new career opens on this screen before the hub. It starts from the last career's look (the default proctor for a first career); bought clothes are not carried. Every choice is free and shows at once on the preview, framed on the head and shoulders. Start career, the ×, and Android back all keep the look and go to the hub; a career left on this screen (the app closed or sent to the background) opens on it again at Continue. The page keeps room under its last row, so every row scrolls clear of the docked button.

The face, brows, hair, facial hair and build choices are a grid: up to four on a line, then three. A long label wraps onto two lines and shrinks a little before anything is cut, which a test checks for every choice in all seven languages at both text sizes.

The You segment

The Wardrobe's last segment, which was Hair, now holds the whole look with the same rows as the creator. A tap saves (there is nothing to buy) and changes only the row it touches; switching Man / Woman refills the rows. On the clothes segments the preview goes back to the whole figure, and try-on works as before.

Looks and builds

Eight looks as the You segment's preview shows them (head and shoulders), then the same eight on a clothes segment (whole figure). Slim and heavy are the art's build_slim / build_heavy blend shapes: they reshape the whole body (torso, shoulders, arms, hips and legs), and every top and trouser cut follows, with the hem and the waistband meeting in every build. Facial hair is hair only, so the face's own mouth shows through the beard and moustache.

Outfits

Wardrobe items worn on the proctor, in seven slots: hat, top, trousers, shoes, badge, glasses and wrist. Tops, trousers and shoes are the art's refits, each top closed with skin at its neckline, sleeves and hem, so no opening shows the inside of the top; the shirt & tie in the room views has the new folded collar; hats bring their own hair-under-hat variant; the badge clips on the upper chest at a spot set per body, with its own spot on the tops whose front opens (the cardigan, blazer, hoodie and shirt and tie) so it stays on plain cloth.

In the room

The proctor in a running exam at Maple, among the students (who keep their own body). The figure stands about 1.8 m with its hair, over the agent, and walks with the kit's own clips. Four looks across two exams, seen from above the game camera and closer in.

The woman walking across the room (average build, bob, shirt and tie, formal trousers, loafers), four frames of a stride. Each clip carries a set of channels for each rig, and the game plays her own set on her rig: her knees stay at least 0.18 m apart and her feet at least 0.16 m apart through a whole stride, and they never cross (a test holds this).

Japanese

Every new word is in all seven languages (written for each, not copied from English), and Japanese is drawn with the bundled Noto Sans JP. The You segment is 外見 (Appearance), as in the glossary.

How it works

The art agent's manifest is the contract: the game reads part, role and shape names from it, never mesh indices, so a new export is three files copied and the prefab built again.

The look

Body, face, brows, hair, facial hair, build, skin, hair colour and eye colour. Face, brows and hair are kept per body, so Man / Woman brings each one's own back; build and colours are shared. Nothing in the simulation reads any of it.

Assembling the figure

Both rigs and every part are in the prefab. Dressing turns on exactly the body's parts, the face, brows, hair (or its under-hat variant), facial hair cut for that face, and each worn item's parts, turning off what an item hides (a top hides the issued tee). Wardrobe parts are attached and bound to the rig's bones by name.

Colour by role

One white material, tinted per surface by its role: skin, hair, eyes, top, legwear and shoes times their factor; stubble lerp(skin, hair, 0.6), the art's factor; a beard or moustache's hair the hair colour with a fifth of the skin mixed in, because a beard is thinner than scalp hair and a pure black one read as a flat mask; facial hair carries no mouth of its own, the face's shows through an opening at the moustache line; the pupil a fixed near-black; each top's skin closures the skin colour; Wardrobe surfaces the item's own three colours.

The preview

One preview stage serves the creator and the Wardrobe. Whichever page is shown binds it to its own picture and framing (head and shoulders for the look, the whole figure for clothes), so returning to a kept Wardrobe after a new career never shows the creator's frozen frame.

Build

Slim and heavy set their blend shape to full on every shown mesh that has it; average is zero. The body, the issued tee, the default legs and every top and trouser cut carry dense shapes, and a test checks that each one has both and that heavy moves it.

Badges

The prefab builder reads the badge rule from the manifest: a height below the shoulders and a side offset per body, and a top's own entry where its front opens. The badge pose is measured per top and per build, so it stays on plain cloth on the upper chest.

A missing item

If an item ever lacks proctor art, it is worn cleanly: the slot keeps nothing visible, a top falls back to the issued tee in the item's colour, and no part of the old student-body wardrobe is ever drawn. Today every item has art.

Words

Titles and buttons at most four words, the creator's note one sentence, the You segment inside the Wardrobe's 120-word budget; every key in English, Turkish, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Japanese.

Saves

No save version bump. The look is a new field: a save without it loads as the default proctor. The old hairstyles are mapped on every load (it is idempotent) to the nearest new one:

Open questions

  • Mohawks. Offered because the art lists them; they read loud for a proctor and can be dropped there.
