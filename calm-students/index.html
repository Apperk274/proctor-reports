Calm Students

Students in the exam room used to be busy all the time. Each one started a new visible gesture every five or six seconds, and the animation stretched a short stretch into five seconds of arms in the air. Now students mostly write. Gestures are rarer and shorter, and the things worth watching stand out. Cheating, raised hands and the sounds the room makes are unchanged, and stillness gives nobody away: honest students and cheaters fidget at the same rate.

What the room looks like now

Recorded on a Galaxy A15 from the calm-students build: about 20 seconds of an exam at each school, starting right after admission. Nobody is steered; this is the ordinary room. The frame rate held at 45 fps throughout, and logcat showed no errors from the game.

No animation pops. The largest single-frame change in each clip, below the timer, is the Buzz toast appearing. Student motion never moved more than 0.3% of the room's pixels in one frame.

Stillness is not a tell

The rule is the same for everyone: it reads no personality and no guilt. It applies only to ordinary and nervous gestures, never to anything a cheat or an innocent pocket, paper or whisper arc does. A test pins the equal rates. Innocent glances (look around) are kept more common than the other gestures, so a glance stays ambiguous.

The sounds are unchanged. Buzzes and whispers come only from arcs this does not touch. A test replays exams with the change on and off and finds every cheat and every buzz at the same instant. Only stretching scrapes a chair, so the scrape's chance per second was raised to keep scrapes per exam where they were (0.44–1.47 heard, against 0.47–1.48 before).

Why they were always moving, and what changed

Ordinary gestures were constant

Stretching, fidgeting, checking the clock, drinking and looking around ran outside every rule that paces the room. Each student rested only 1–3.5 s between them, and writing was a fifth of the choices.

Now: 80% of those picks become writing (50% for look around). The ones that remain are 0.7× as long.

The animation stretched them

A stretch of 1–2 s played its whole 5.4 s clip. A third of the students cycled through an erasing clip, and idle students swung their arms through a reading clip.

Now: the stretch plays at 2.5× (about 2.1 s, the whole gesture). Writing uses only the two still writing clips, and idle uses only the calm idle.

Kept as they were

  • Every cheating route, the innocent pockets, papers and whispers, raised hands and bathroom requests
  • The room's pace for incidents (quiet spell and simultaneous incidents by room size)
  • Animation blend times: shorter fades were tried and made a raised hand drop 7–8 cm in one frame
