Exam Supervisor Simulator · reward motion

Game Feel

When something good happens, the game now shows it arriving instead of swapping a number. A salary flies into your balance as coins, a result card counts up what you earned, and every action you take in the room says that it landed. The motion is short and plays once, so it never makes you wait. All clips below are from Play mode in the editor at phone resolution.

What moves

The moments in the order a player meets them: an action in the room, the result, payday, then the milestones and decisions between exams.

An action lands

Warning, confiscating or removing someone shows one short line under the clock for about 2.6 seconds. It settles in and fades out.

  • It says that you acted, never whether you were right. The wording comes from the game's existing feedback text, which deliberately leaves judgement to later consequences.
  • Restored, not new. The game already wrote this line after every action, but nothing had drawn it since the old IMGUI HUD was removed. Players only heard a chime.
  • It takes no taps, so it never blocks a press meant for a student.

The result card counts what you earned

Money used to be a line of text under the rows. Each payment is now its own row, for example Salary +80 ¢. When the card opens, the rows arrive one after another, and the salary and reputation numbers count up from zero. A gain gets a single pop on its icon when the count finishes.

  • It plays only when the card opens. A redraw shows the final numbers, so the card never flickers.
  • Costs, such as monthly expenses, show as they are: in red and without counting.

Payday: coins fly to your balance

The status bar is hidden during the exam and on the result card, so the salary is always earned while it is away. When you return to the hub, the difference is paid in: three to eight coins fly from the middle of the screen to the balance. The balance counts up as each coin lands, and the bar pops once when the last one arrives.

  • Reputation rolls too. The number counts to its new value, its colour fades across, and a gain pops once.
  • Losses stay calm. Spending in the Store, or a fine, rolls the number down with no coins and no pop.
  • A new or continued career shows its balance as it is rather than "earning" it on the first screen. The first salary of a new career still flies.

A promotion is marked once

The first time the hub shows a better posting, one soft band of brass light crosses its picture and the picture pops once. A distinction at the top posting gets the same mark.

  • One sweep, never again. It plays on the first hub that shows the new posting and not on later visits.
  • A demotion gets nothing. The new building says enough.
  • The light passes under the words, inside the picture's frame, and takes no taps.

The career ring moves from where you last saw it

The progress ring on the Career tab sweeps from the value it showed last time to the value it has now. After a bad stretch it goes down as readily as up.

  • Redraws stay still. Once you have seen a value, it is drawn where it is.
  • Tabs now arrive too. Career, Store, Settings and the Manual share one screen shell, so switching between them used to swap the cards in a single frame. They now arrive with the same entrance as the hub.

Decisions and purchases

  • An event answer lands. The answer you chose stays lit and stamps once. The others fade out where they stand instead of disappearing, so the chosen button no longer jumps up the card while you are looking at it.
  • A purchase pops. In the Store, the item you just bought pops once in its new, owned state, while the balance rolls down.

The rules it follows

These are written into the design system guide (Docs/Design/UI-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md, §Motion), and the tests check them.

State first

The game has already applied the change before anything moves. Animation only shows it. Nothing waits for it, and taps always go through.

Once, then still

A gain rolls, pops or flies once. Nothing loops or pulses, and nothing glows on its own.

Warm for gains, calm for losses

Only gains get coins and a pop. A cost rolls down quietly: no shake, no red flash.

Reduce motion

A new switch in Settings → Display and help. With it on, every number simply shows its new value.

How it is tuned

All timings are theme tokens in GameTheme.cs. No screen hardcodes any of them.

During the exam

Kept to the minimum, because the room has to stay readable. Nothing here says whether a decision was right.

The clock turns warm for the last 30 seconds

The bar and the time ease once from blue to amber and stay there. It never pulses or flashes: it says "not long now" without blinking while you are deciding about a student.

In the room

A removed student walks out

Removing someone used to make them vanish from their chair. They now stand, walk up the aisle and leave through the door, in about four seconds.

  • Removed still means removed. The moment you remove them, they can no longer be tapped, and once they are through the door they are gone exactly as before.
  • Everyone leaves the same way, at the same pace, so the walk says nothing about whether they were guilty.
  • It plays only at the moment of removal: never again after loading a save, and not at all with Reduce motion on. If the bell rings mid-walk, they are simply gone.

Left out on purpose

  • No "Time is up" banner. The bell ends the exam and the result card opens in the same moment, so there is nothing to show it over.
  • No extra reaction to a warning. A student already turns to face the supervisor who walks up to them, which is the moment a warning happens.
