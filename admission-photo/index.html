Admission Photo Check

At the door, the player compares the photo on a candidate's document with the person in front of them. This used to be four lines of words per side. It is now two pictures: the document photo, rendered from the photo's traits in a plain booth, and a still, live camera on the spot where the room walks each student to the door. The player compares faces, not words: the person's look is no longer written under their name. Everything below is a capture of the real admission screen at 1080×2340 portrait. The photos were forced to each case on a fixed-seed career.

Verdict

Every impostor change is visible at phone size, with the camera now fixed in place. The face at the door is about as large as it was when the camera followed the person, and it is seen a little more from the side (26° instead of 22°). Hair and glasses read at a glance. Eyes and skin need a real look, which is the judgement the door is meant to ask for. Two pairs are close enough that a player could miss them.

at a glance

Haircut, hair colour, glasses

Obvious every time. These are the ordinary changes a genuine holder can show.

with a look

Eyes

The eyes are large painted discs. Green, blue and hazel stand apart. Brown against hazel is the subtlest, more so behind glasses or on darker skin.

with a look

Skin, most pairs

Medium against tan, tan against dark, and anything two steps apart are clear.

with care

Skin, light against medium

The weakest case. The photo's even light and the room's warm light shift both tones a little. It reads, but only if you compare the lit cheeks. The camera stays on the side of the face the room's sun lights, so a light face does not turn grey.

The screen

  • No look in words. The line under the name used to print "long auburn hair, brown eyes, light skin, glasses". It is hidden whenever the pictures show. It comes back only in the game's developer debug mode (the same switch as the ground-truth labels, which a release build cannot turn on), and when the pictures cannot render, where the card falls back to words.
  • Document photo. Head and shoulders, dead on, an even frontal light, a pale backdrop. The sitter always wears the same grey top, so clothing never enters the comparison. Rendered once per face and cached.
  • At the door. A live camera that never moves. It is placed once from the spot where the room walks each student to be checked, at standing eye height, a little in front of them and 26° to the side the room's sun lights. It does not follow anyone. The person being checked fills the right of the frame. The open door at the back of the room is behind them, so the walk in and the way out show behind and beside them.
  • The rest of the card is unchanged. The "Photo" row keeps its label and the pictures replace its two value cells.

Coming and going

Four frames from the live picture after a decision, camera untouched. Captured with the game slowed to a quarter speed so the walk shows in four frames; at full speed the change-over takes about a second.

The queue at the door

The students waiting to be checked stand in one evenly spaced line. It starts at the back of the room, runs along the back wall and goes out through the open door. Nobody stands in a desk or on top of anybody else. Captured in Play mode at 1080×2340 with six students, with the card hidden to show the room.

  • One line, evenly spaced. The places are 0.75 m apart. The head of the line stands at the back of the room and faces the inspection spot. Everyone else faces the person ahead of them.
  • Out of the door. Four places fit inside the room. The last one is just beside the door. Anyone after the fourth waits in the corridor behind the wall, each in their own place. Cohorts are 3 to 6 students, so with 4 or 5 waiting the line reaches the door.
  • Clear of the furniture. The line is 1.1 m behind the back-row chairs and 0.5 m in front of the wall. Before, the queue lane ran through the back-left desk. Everyone past the second place stood on one point.
  • The body is where the place is. The animated students stand 0.25 m in front of their own root, so that a student who stands up at their seat stands in front of the chair. The old queue spots ignored this, which pushed each body further into the desk. Each root is now placed 0.25 m behind the body it carries.
  • The door opens for admission. The door moved from the left edge of the frame to fully inside it. It now has a frame, a dark doorway and a leaf on a hinge. The leaf swings into the room against the left side when admission starts and closes when the exam begins.

Genuine

The photo matches the holder. Different angle and light, same face.

Drifted photos

The holder, photographed before one ordinary change. A question, not an answer: the face still matches.

Somebody else whose eye colour differs from the holder's, sometimes with a restyle on top.

Impostors: skin

Somebody else whose skin tone differs from the holder's by one step.

Open questions

  • Light against medium skin. It is the smallest step in the skin palette. The steps could be evened out, which also changes how everyone looks in the room, or it could stay as the hard case. A device playtest should decide.
  • Cost on the phone. The live picture renders the room a second time while the door is open, at 320×400 with no shadows or post-processing. That has not been measured on the Galaxy A15 yet.
  • Faces in the background. With a full room, the last student in line shows beside the head of the one being checked, smaller and further back, and a rejected student can be seen leaving through the door behind them. That is the intent, but a playtest should confirm nobody compares the photo with the wrong face.
  • The admitted student passes close to the camera. For a fraction of a second their head fills part of the frame as they walk to their seat. It reads as someone walking past; the camera does not react.
