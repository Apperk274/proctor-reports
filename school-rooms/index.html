Exam Supervisor Simulator · M130

School Rooms

Every school now has its own exam room, dressed for the kind of place it is. Better-rated schools have more desks. A tablet gets a bigger room than a phone. The camera zooms out until the whole class is in view, so in a bigger room the people look smaller, and that is where the extra difficulty comes from. One desk is always left free, so a student can be moved. Each capture below shows a full room: every desk except the free one is taken. They come from Play mode with the real HUD, at a phone size (1080 × 2340) and two tablet sizes (1620 × 2160 and 2048 × 2732).

The rooms

The students column is for a school exam, which fills 75–100% of the room's capacity (desks − 1). An entrance exam fills 60–85% and a certification 45–70%. The largest cohort is always at most desks − 1. Code enforces this, and tests check every school × exam × device.

On a phone

1080 × 2340. From 6 desks at Maple up to 21 at the National Examination Centre.

On a tablet

1620 × 2160, a 10.2-inch iPad-class screen. From 9 desks up to 35. A wider screen holds more columns, and a bigger screen keeps people readable.

The same rooms at 2048 × 2732 (12.9-inch) look the same and slightly larger, because the screen has the same shape and more pixels.

Leaning in still works

The M113.1 interaction focus is unchanged. When you walk to a candidate, the camera leans in on them from the zoomed-out view, in any room. In the National hall the farthest candidate grows from 156 to 545 px on a phone and from 131 to 517 px on a 10.2-inch tablet, and sits at 0.62 of the screen height, above the action card.

How the sizes were chosen

Readability floor95 / 84 px

The minimum height of the farthest seated body: 95 px on a 1080 × 2340 phone and 84 px on a 1620 × 2160 tablet. Both are the same visual angle, about 1.15°: 6 mm at 30 cm on a phone, 8 mm at 40 cm on a tablet.

Largest room21 / 35

The largest exam hall that stays above the floor, as calibrated: 3 × 7 desks on a phone and 5 × 7 on a tablet. One more row or column falls below the floor, and a test checks that.

Camerafit all

The camera position is solved exactly from the room and the screen shape. Every desk and body is in frame, clear of the HUD, from as close as possible. It is re-solved only when the room or the screen shape changes.

Device classshape + size

The device class is decided from the screen's shape and physical size, not the platform. A screen counts as a tablet if it is at most 1.8:1 and at least 7 inches across. That covers Android tablets, unfolded foldables and iPads alike.

The room is now where the difficulty comes from. Maple is as easy as before, and the top schools are much harder, more so on a tablet. Standing still rises at every school. A careful 21-exam career ends with less money: about 730 coins instead of about 1,300 (median). That is worth a design decision.

Cost

  • Simulation: 0.5 µs per step with 5 candidates, 1.9 µs with 20 and 3.6 µs with 34 (headless benchmark). Negligible.
  • Rendering in the editor: 0.7 ms per frame with 5 candidates, 1.3 ms with 20 and 2.3 ms with 34. It grows roughly linearly with the number of bodies. This is not a device measurement.
  • Not yet verified: the frame rate on a real phone or tablet, and how readable the smallest rooms' signals are to a human in a playtest.
