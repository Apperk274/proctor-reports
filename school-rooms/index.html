School Rooms

Every school now has its own exam room, dressed for the kind of place it is. Better-rated schools have more desks. A tablet gets a bigger room than a phone. The camera zooms out until the whole class is in view. A bigger school has more people to watch, and each of them looks smaller. The room's pace also scales with the number of people in it, so a hall is busier than a classroom without becoming overwhelming. One desk is always left free, so a student can be moved. Each capture below shows a full room: every desk except the free one is taken. They come from Play mode with the real HUD, at a phone size (1080 × 2340) and two tablet sizes (1620 × 2160 and 2048 × 2732).

The rooms

The students columns are for a school exam, which fills 80–100% of the room's capacity (desks − 1). An entrance exam fills 65–90% and a certification 50–75%. The largest cohort is always at most desks − 1. Code enforces this, and tests check every school × exam × device.

On a phone

1080 × 2340. From 5 desks at Maple up to 12 at the National Examination Centre.

On a tablet

1620 × 2160, a 10.2-inch iPad-class screen. From 6 desks up to 16.

The same rooms at 2048 × 2732 (12.9-inch) look the same and slightly larger, because the screen has the same shape and more pixels.

Leaning in still works

The M113.1 interaction focus is unchanged. When you walk to a candidate, the camera leans in on them from the zoomed-out view, in any room. In the National hall the farthest candidate grows from 169 to 524 px on a phone and from 182 to 517 px on a 10.2-inch tablet, and sits at 0.62 of the screen height, above the action card.

How the sizes were chosen

Desk counts5 → 12 / 6 → 16

Phone and tablet desk counts, ordered by the school's rating. Schools with the same rating get the same count. This is an approved design table. A first version sized rooms to what a screen can legibly show (21 and 35 desks), and that was too many for one supervisor.

Readability floor95 / 84 px

The floor is now a safety bound, not the target. The farthest seated body must be at least 95 px tall on a 1080 × 2340 phone and 84 px on a 1620 × 2160 tablet. Every room clears it by at least 15%: the smallest is 113 px on a phone and 118 px on a tablet.

Room pacescaled

Above three people, the quiet spell after an incident gets longer with the size of the room. One more incident may run at once per five people, and from ten people there is a third raised hand. So the room is busier at any moment, while incidents per exam grow slowly.

Camerafit all

The camera position is solved exactly from the room and the screen shape. Every desk and body is in frame, clear of the HUD, from as close as possible.

Device classshape + size

A screen counts as a tablet if it is at most 1.8:1 and at least 7 inches across. This covers Android tablets, unfolded foldables and iPads. The platform name is never used.

Cost

  • Simulation: 0.5 µs per step with 4–5 candidates, 1.0 µs with 11 and 1.4 µs with 15 (headless benchmark). Negligible.
  • Rendering in the editor: 0.6 ms per frame for Maple and 1.1–1.4 ms for the 16-desk tablet hall. This is not a device measurement.
