Career Balance

This report covers how fast a career climbs from Maple to the National Examination Centre, how easily it moves between schools, what it earns and when the store is bought. A whole-game audit found the climb too fast and promotions too easy, and proposed three tuning packages. The chosen package, Target, is now built into the game's career rules and measured with those shipped values. The new store prices arrive separately, in the store's own update.

Each figure comes from 36 bot careers × 100 exams per player type, on two seed blocks. Careers start at Maple with the tutorial on, in phone rooms, with a tablet arm for the key numbers. The player types are a careful player, a careful player who also inspects, a beginner careful by exam 10, a beginner careful by exam 20, an error-prone player, and a careful player who takes the money. "Before" is the game as it was before this change.

What changed

  • A minimum stay before any job offer: 6 exams at Maple, Riverside and Eastgate, 10 at City High and Brightpath, and 30 at Kingsley and State University. The count starts at the last promotion or demotion. Moving to an equally rated school that pays more does not restart it.
  • One rung at a time. A promotion goes to the next better-rated school that pays more, never further: Maple → Riverside → Eastgate → City High → Kingsley → National. From Brightpath the next rung is Kingsley.
  • A safe start at a new school. For its first 6 exams a new posting cannot be lost.
  • Standing matters at the top. A good exam earns +1 standing at Kingsley, State University and National, and +2 below them.
  • The standing each school asks is re-spaced (Riverside 46 up to National 85), and the standing at which you lose a school now sits 12–16 below it.
  • Pay moves toward the start of the career: Maple 55, Riverside 65, Eastgate 75, City High 90, Brightpath 115, Kingsley 120, State 135, National 155.
  • A slower top school: career rewards come every 3 exams there instead of every 2, and living costs are 150 / 220 / 290 by level of school.
  • School crests are earned. Ten exams at a school earn its crest, delivered on the usual reward card. A crest you already bought is yours to keep.

The Career screen will show "N more exams here before the next offer" and the safe-start count. The Wardrobe will show "4 / 10 exams at Riverside". Both come with the store's own update.

  • Every stage now has something next: the next school on a visible count, a crest after ten exams, and standing that still moves at the top.
  • Still open: after about exam 70–87 there is nothing new left to aim for. About 4.5k coins are left over with nothing to spend them on. Fixing that needs a late goal or something late to buy, which needs new screens.

Caveats

  • Bots are not players. They never use tools or shop, and the beginner's learning curve is a straight line from error-prone to careful.
  • Careful pace is now a clock: everyone who plays well reaches the top at exam 58.
  • Saves keep working without migration. An existing career reads as having moved long ago, so no minimum stay or safe start applies to it. The store's own update will credit the exams already played at each school toward crests.
  • The two seed blocks agree within a few exams on every careful pace figure and within about 5% on coins.
