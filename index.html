Exam Supervisor Simulator

Proctor Simulator — Reports

Visual review reports from the game's development.

  • 2026-10-27

    Exam Bell Sounds

    Fourteen bells for the moment exam time runs out, all synthesized in code: electric school bells, a large struck bell, a shaken hand bell, PA chimes, a tubular bell, a Westminster phrase, a digital chime and a desk bell. Listen to each, with its parameters and measurements; the electric school bell is the one in the game now.
  • 2026-10-27

    School Rooms

    Each of the eight schools has its own exam room, dressed for the kind of place it is. Rooms grow with the school's rating and are bigger on tablets, and the camera zooms out to show the whole class. Captures on a phone and two tablet sizes, how the sizes were calibrated, and what it does to difficulty.
  • 2026-10-27

    Whisper Sound

    The whisper in the game: real public-domain whispers, rebuilt so no words come through, in a male and a female voice matched to the student and played from the one who is whispering. Both pools with audio, how it plays, and the measurements.
  • 2026-10-27

    Baked Game Art

    The game's pictures as Blender renders: a building for each of the eight schools, the exam room on the title screen and brief, rendered event objects and five redrawn icons, in the dark and light themes at phone size.
  • 2026-10-26

    Schools and Offers

    Eight schools with fixed star ratings, offers that compare rating and salary now against the school offered, two schools on one card, the lower-rated school that pays more, and how school rating follows standing over whole careers.
  • 2026-10-27

    Whispering Students

    Two seated clips, a student who whispers and a neighbour who listens, leaning in and turning to each other, shown at the game's camera and up close; and the Blender against Meshy comparison that led to the choice.
  • 2026-10-27

    The Way Out

    A student removed during the exam walks the aisle and goes out through the door, which opens for them and closes behind them, instead of walking inside the back wall. Before and after, and the route from every seat.
  • 2026-10-27

    Admission Photo Check

    The photo check at the door as pictures: the document photo beside a still, live camera on the person standing there, every case captured on the phone screen, how readable each one is, and the line of students waiting at the open door.
  • 2026-10-25

    Game Feel

    The reward motion: coins flying to your balance on payday, a result card that counts up, a promotion marked with light, and a line confirming each action in the room.
  • 2026-10-27

    Student Characters

    The exam students' models and animations: who they are, what they wear, every gesture, the whispering pair beside each other in a real exam, and the known limits.
