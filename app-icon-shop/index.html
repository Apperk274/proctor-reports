App icon

The user chose the Angled room from four scene candidates, with every student blue: a quiet exam room, with no cheater picked out.

An Android adaptive icon is 108 dp. The launcher shows the central 72 dp through its own mask and guarantees only the central 66 dp circle (green in the layer view). The room runs to the edges, so every mask trims a different part of it and the middle stays whole.

The exam room on the app-icon branch

Six students from behind, at wooden desks with white papers, on a blue floor. Each of the three layers (floor, desks and papers, students) has its own lightness, so the room survives being shrunk. The floor is lifted off navy so the icon keeps its edge on a dark wallpaper.

At 48 px: it reads as rows of people at desks, and its edge holds on both wallpapers. Single desks and papers are not distinct at that size; the regular pattern carries it.

Shop tiles in the game

The Store, captured from the game. Phone Detector, Pills, the Navy, Charcoal and Slate shirts and the Bronze, Silver, Enamel, Supporter and Regional badges are Blender renders. Replay keeps its current drawing. The "You are not well" illness picture is not part of this work.

A render can't be tinted without muddying it, so each tile shows its state another way. If you don't own an item, its art is dimmed. If it is carried or worn, a green check sits in the corner of the art, and the word ("Carried", "Worn") is on the button. In these captures the Detector is carried and Replay is not owned. The Navy Shirt is worn. The Charcoal Shirt and Silver Badge are not owned. The Bronze Badge, Slate Shirt and Enamel Badge are owned but not worn.

Where things live

  • App icon. Assets/Art/Branding holds the adaptive background (the whole scene), an empty foreground and the flattened AppIcon.png (the 72 dp view at 1024 px). Proctor.EditorTools.Branding.Apply, run with the Android target, fills the Legacy, Round and Adaptive slots. The 512 px Play Store icon is ArtSource/Branding/PlayStoreIcon512.png.
  • Shop tiles. Props.TileArt(itemId, …) loads Resources/Art/Props/tile_<id> (480 × 230, about 0.06 MB each at ASTC 6×6). An id with no render falls back to the drawn tile without an error, so a new cosmetic never shows a blank tile. To add one, add a line to the TILES table in shop.py and render it.

Sources: ArtSource/UI/imgq/blender/icon_scene.py (the angled scene) and shop.py; make_branding.py, compose_icons.py and build_icon_shop_report.py. The Store captures come from the PlayMode test StoreTileCaptures.
