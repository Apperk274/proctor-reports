App Icon and Shop Art

Candidates for the launcher icon and the seven shop tiles, in the Blender style chosen for the game's scenes. Each is shown at the size a phone shows it, beside what the game ships today.

Icons: four concepts. Each is rendered as a 1024 px adaptive-icon foreground on a transparent background and placed on a background layer. Each is then shown through the circle, squircle and rounded-square launcher masks at 192, 96 and 48 px, and in a home-screen row. Shop: each item as a still life on the same desk as the event objects. It is shown in a Store tile at the size StoreScreen draws it (the art panel is 376 × 180 px on a 1080-wide screen), with the current tile first.

Icon → D, the eye

An eye looking through a magnifier at an exam paper. It says "someone is watching the exam", and the eye is still readable at 48 px. C, the supervisor's ID badge, is the runner-up.

Shop → all seven

Every Blender tile reads as the object it sells. The current tiles are glyphs on a dark band. Shirts on a hanger beat folded shirts, and the pill bottle beats the blister pack.

Cost

About 0.06 MB per shop tile at ASTC 6×6 and about 0.2 MB of launcher images for the icon. No Meshy credits were used.

App icon

An Android adaptive icon is two 108 dp layers. The launcher shows the central 72 dp through its own mask and guarantees only a 66 dp circle, so every subject sits inside that circle (green in the layer view). Each concept is shown in the same order: the icon, the two layers, the three masks at three sizes on a light and a dark wallpaper, and the home-screen row.

Current shipped

A document and a magnifying glass, drawn flat. It is clear, but generic: it could be any file or search app.

A · Clipboard and magnifier

A checklist with four green ticks under a magnifying glass. It is the current idea made solid and given a job: checking. At 48 px the ticks merge into a green stripe, but the shape still reads as a clipboard.

B · Exam paper and stopwatch

A marked exam paper with a brass stopwatch in front. It reads as "timed exam" at once, and the brass is the game's prestige accent. It says less about supervising, though: a study-timer app could use the same icon.

C · Supervisor's ID badge

The staff badge an invigilator wears: a photo, an eye emblem, a blue band and a lanyard. It is the most "you are the supervisor" of the four, and the white card stands out on any wallpaper. The lanyard runs out of the safe zone on purpose and is cut by every mask, which is fine.

D · The watchful eye recommended

An eye looks through a magnifying glass at an exam paper. It is the one image that says "watching an exam" rather than "an exam" or "a file". The eye is the largest, highest-contrast shape, so it survives at 48 px and under all three masks.

Shop tiles

The coin store sells two tools (Phone Detector, Replay), four cosmetics (Navy Shirt, Charcoal Shirt, Bronze Badge, Silver Badge) and, in its Medicine section, Pills. In each row below, the first tile is the current one and the others are Blender candidates. All are dark theme, with the light theme under each row. The illness event picture is not part of this; another session is replacing it.

Phone Detector

Blender. A handheld scanner with lit signal bars and an antenna: plainly a detector, where the current one is an icon of a phone.

Replay

Blender. The tool recalls the last few seconds, sounds included. A pocket recorder with a waveform and rewind keys says that better than a rewind arrow does.

Navy Shirt

Hanger (third). The folded shirt reads as a box from this angle; on a hanger it is unmistakably a shirt.

Charcoal Shirt

Hanger. Navy and charcoal stay distinguishable side by side. The render uses each shirt's real colour, lifted a little the way the Store's swatch does.

Bronze Badge

Blender. A shield pin in the badge's own metal, with an enamel field and a raised star.

Silver Badge

Blender. The same shield in silver, clearly a different metal from the bronze.

Pills

Bottle (third). The amber bottle with a green cross reads as medicine at tile size. The blister pack is fine but small.

What implementing the chosen ones involves

  • App icon (Android).
    • Render the chosen foreground and background layers at 1024 px and replace AppIconForeground.png, AppIconBackground.png and the flattened AppIcon.png in Assets/Art/Branding.
    • Re-run Proctor.EditorTools.Branding.Apply in batchmode with the Android target. It fills the Legacy, Round and Adaptive icon slots and writes them into ProjectSettings.
    • The Store listing needs a 512 px PNG of the same icon.
    • Size: Unity generates the mipmap-density PNGs, about 0.2 MB in total.
  • App icon (iOS, later). One flattened 1024 px icon with no transparency. Unity makes the other sizes. The rounded corners come from iOS, so the composite is used as it is.
  • Shop tiles.
    • Render the seven at 480 × 230 into Assets/Resources/Art/Props/. BakedArtImporter already handles that folder (no mipmaps, ASTC 6×6).
    • Props.Get(prop, tint, PropFrame.Tile) loads them the way the event banners already do. Each colour of shirt and badge is its own render, so the tile is keyed by the cosmetic id, not by Prop.
    • Size: about 0.06 MB each, roughly 0.45 MB for all seven.
  • A design decision for the shop. Today the tile art is tinted to show state: dimmed when not owned, bright when equipped. A render cannot be tinted without muddying it, so state should move to the tile itself, for example a dimmed panel or an "Equipped" chip. The design system already has those components.
  • Pack-only cosmetics (Slate Shirt and the Enamel, Supporter and Regional badges) appear only once owned. Each needs a render too: four more, using the same scripts with a colour change.
