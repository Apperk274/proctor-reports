App icon: a scene

The chosen direction is a tiny exam room with one student cheating, singled out. An Android adaptive icon is 108 dp. The launcher shows the central 72 dp through its own mask and guarantees only the central 66 dp circle (green in the layer view), so each subject sits inside that circle while the room runs to the edges.

The first round of concepts (A clipboard, B stopwatch, C ID badge, D eye) was rejected.

Current shipped

A document and a magnifying glass, drawn flat. It is clear but generic: it could be any file or search app.

1 · Top-down recommended

Four big desks seen from straight above. Three students in blue sit still. The fourth, in orange, leans toward the next paper under a tight warm spotlight. There are few shapes and they are large, which is why it holds up when small. The circle mask trims the corners of the outer desks, but the cheater stays inside the safe circle.

At 48 px: the clearest of the four. Four squares, one of them bright: it says “one of these is cheating” at a glance.

2 · Angled room

A 3 × 2 room in three-quarter view, in cool blue. The cheater glows orange in the front row and leans toward a neighbour. At full size it looks the most like the game.

At 48 px: the orange student survives, but the room around it becomes noise.

3 · The note

Two desks close up in warm light, with a glowing note being passed between the students. The supervisor is implied by a long shadow that falls across the floor from behind the viewer.

At 48 px: the colour is distinctive but the story is lost: the note and the shadow are too small to read.

4 · Over the shoulder

The supervisor's view: a dark shoulder and head in the near corner, four desks beyond, and a dotted line tracing the cheater's glance to the next paper.

At 48 px: the glance line disappears. This is the weakest at launcher size.

Shop tiles shipped on the branch

The Store, captured from the game. Phone Detector, Pills, the Navy, Charcoal and Slate shirts and the Bronze, Silver, Enamel, Supporter and Regional badges are Blender renders. Replay keeps its current drawing. The "You are not well" illness picture is not part of this work.

A render can't be tinted without muddying it, so each tile shows its state another way. If you don't own an item, its art is dimmed. If it is carried or worn, a green check sits in the corner of the art, and the word ("Carried", "Worn") is on the button. In these captures the Detector is carried and Replay is not owned. The Navy Shirt is worn. The Charcoal Shirt and Silver Badge are not owned. The Bronze Badge, Slate Shirt and Enamel Badge are owned but not worn.

Where things stand

  • Shop tiles. Props.TileArt(itemId, …) loads Resources/Art/Props/tile_<id> (480 × 230, about 0.06 MB each at ASTC 6×6). An id with no render falls back to the drawn tile without an error, so a new cosmetic never shows a blank tile. To add one, add a line to the TILES table in shop.py and render it.
  • App icon. If Top-down is chosen: render it at 1024 px, replace the files in Assets/Art/Branding, and re-run Proctor.EditorTools.Branding.Apply for Android. The Store listing also needs a 512 px PNG. First, it would help to lift the background off pure navy so the icon keeps an edge on dark wallpapers.

Sources on the art-icon-shop branch: ArtSource/UI/imgq/blender/icon_scene.py and shop.py, composed by compose_icons.py and build_icon_shop_report.py. The Store captures come from the PlayMode test StoreTileCaptures.
