App Icon and Shop Art

The game's launcher icon at the sizes a phone shows it, eight simpler alternatives that read at a glance, and the Blender shop tiles as the Store draws them.

Icon: one full-bleed 1024 px render used as the adaptive icon's background layer, with an empty foreground. It is shown through the circle, squircle and rounded-square masks at 192, 96 and 48 px on a light and a dark wallpaper, and in a home-screen row. It is a whole classroom, too busy to recognise at launcher size, so Icon alternatives tries six bolder single-idea directions and two colour variants, each next to the current icon at true size, under four masks and on a mock home screen. Shop: the Store, captured from the game at 1080 × 2340 in both themes.

Tests

EditMode and PlayMode both pass. No Meshy credits were used.

App icon

The user chose the Angled room from four scene candidates, with every student blue: a quiet exam room, with no cheater picked out.

An Android adaptive icon is 108 dp. The launcher shows the central 72 dp through its own mask and guarantees only the central 66 dp circle (green in the layer view). The room runs to the edges, so every mask trims a different part of it and the middle stays whole.

The exam room on the app-icon branch

Six students from behind, at wooden desks with white papers, on a blue floor. Each of the three layers (floor, desks and papers, students) has its own lightness, so the room survives being shrunk. The floor is lifted off navy so the icon keeps its edge on a dark wallpaper.

At 48 px: its edge holds on both wallpapers, but the room turns into a blue texture. Single students, desks and papers are not distinct, and in a grid of other apps you can't tell what it is without the label. That is why the alternatives below exist.

Icon alternatives

Six simpler directions and two colour variants. Each one has a single focal idea, a few large shapes and strong contrast, and no text. Most lean on the working title Pencils Down!. They are concepts for review: the game still ships the exam room above.

Every concept is a real adaptive icon: a background and a foreground layer on a 108 dp canvas, with the subject inside the 66 dp safe circle, plus a flat 512 px Play Store version. All of them were drawn by a script. No generator credits were used.

Real size. The size strips, the 48 px line-up and the phones are drawn at 2× and shown at half size. On a wide enough screen a “48 px” icon really is 48 CSS px, about what a phone shows at arm’s length. The home screens are 360 dp wide: a 4 × 5 grid of generic placeholder apps, with the concept in the third row. The light wallpaper uses circle masks and the dark one squircle masks. Every mock-up labels the app “Pencils Down!”, including the current icon, so the label doesn’t favour any of them.

Side-Eye Recommended · 1st

The supervisor as a face: big round glasses, eyes glancing sideways, one brow down and one raised. A suspicious look, on brass yellow.

At launcher size: Nothing else on a home screen looks like it. The two lenses and the brows still hold at 32 px. The ring of dark frames gives a strong edge on both wallpapers. It is a character rather than an object, so it also says “game”.

Side-Eye, blue Colour variant

The same face on the game’s Primary blue instead of brass.

At launcher size: Closer to the game’s own palette. On a light wallpaper it reads as well as the yellow one, but it is quieter in a grid full of blue icons (Messages, Browser, Games). Yellow is the better choice.

Pencil ! Recommended · 2nd

An exclamation mark built from a chunky yellow pencil, tip down, with a white dot. The brief’s one glyph.

At launcher size: The boldest silhouette in the set: one vertical stroke and a dot, on red. It is the most “Pencils Down!” of all, almost a logo. On its own it says “alert” or “warning” more than “exam”, so it leans on the name.

Pencil Down Recommended · 3rd

A yellow pencil laid down across a tilted exam sheet, on the game’s blue.

At launcher size: The most literal reading of the name, and clean at 48 px: a white sheet and a yellow diagonal. It is also the most generic. Notes and editor apps use the same picture, so it says “writing app” before “exam game”.

Pencil Down, navy Colour variant

The same picture on the game’s navy ground, with a soft blue glow.

At launcher size: Matches the in-game UI best, and the white sheet pops more on navy than on blue. On a dark wallpaper the navy edge disappears and only the sheet is left. The blue version keeps its shape.

Watchful Eye

One wide-open eye looking down at an exam paper, on navy.

At launcher size: Very legible: the white almond and blue iris read at 32 px, and the paper strip says “exam” at 96 px and up. The mood is surveillance rather than comedy. It could pass for a privacy or security app.

Time’s Up

A stopwatch whose hand is a pencil, pointing straight up at zero. The red ring says time is up.

At launcher size: Clear and friendly, and it ties “time’s up” to “pencils down”. It reads as a timer first. Next to a Clock app it looks like a sibling, and the pencil hand is just a yellow line at 48 px.

Bubble Sheet

A multiple-choice answer sheet close up: two rows of bubbles, two filled in, a pencil filling the second.

At launcher size: “Exam” for anyone who has sat a bubble test, and the green stands out. It is the busiest of the set. At 32 px the bubbles become dots and it can read as dice or dominoes.

Recommendation

  1. Side-Eye (yellow). It stands out most in a crowded grid and holds at 32 px. It is the only one that shows the player’s role, the suspicious supervisor, rather than an exam object, and it works under either name. Of the eight it is also the only one that looks like a game rather than a utility.
  2. Pencil ! The strongest pure silhouette, and practically a logo for “Pencils Down!”. Choose it if the name changes. Under the current name it is just an alert sign.
  3. Pencil Down (blue). The safest and most literal “exam” picture, in the product’s own blue. It is clean at every size but generic: it competes with every notes app.

Fit with the name. With Pencils Down!: Pencil !, Pencil Down, Time’s Up and Bubble Sheet all illustrate the title. With Exam Supervisor Simulator: Side-Eye and Watchful Eye show the supervisor. Side-Eye is the one that fits both.

Tried and dropped: a raised “stop” hand (clear, but it says “stop” or “high five”, not “exam”), and a cheat note under a spotlight (the beam turned to a grey smudge at 48 px and the note read as a generic notes app).

Shop tiles in the game

The Store, captured from the game. Phone Detector, Pills, the Navy, Charcoal and Slate shirts and the Bronze, Silver, Enamel, Supporter and Regional badges are Blender renders. Replay keeps its current drawing. The "You are not well" illness picture is not part of this work.

A render can't be tinted without muddying it, so each tile shows its state another way. If you don't own an item, its art is dimmed. If it is carried or worn, a green check sits in the corner of the art, and the word ("Carried", "Worn") is on the button. In these captures the Detector is carried and Replay is not owned. The Navy Shirt is worn. The Charcoal Shirt and Silver Badge are not owned. The Bronze Badge, Slate Shirt and Enamel Badge are owned but not worn.

Where things live

  • App icon. Assets/Art/Branding holds the adaptive background (the whole scene), an empty foreground and the flattened AppIcon.png (the 72 dp view at 1024 px). Proctor.EditorTools.Branding.Apply, run with the Android target, fills the Legacy, Round and Adaptive slots. The 512 px Play Store icon is ArtSource/Branding/PlayStoreIcon512.png.
  • Shop tiles. Props.TileArt(itemId, …) loads Resources/Art/Props/tile_<id> (480 × 230, about 0.06 MB each at ASTC 6×6). An id with no render falls back to the drawn tile without an error, so a new cosmetic never shows a blank tile. To add one, add a line to the TILES table in shop.py and render it.
