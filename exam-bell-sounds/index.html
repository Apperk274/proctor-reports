Exam Bell Sounds

When exam time runs out, a bell rings once. It used to be a single sine tone sliding down, which sounded like a button press. Below are fifteen bells to choose from, all built from scratch in code: no recordings and no generated audio. Number 15, the small gong of No. 02 struck at the speed of No. 01, is the one in the game now.

In the game now: Electric bell — small gong, classic speed

No. 02's smaller, higher gong (the sound chosen by ear) at No. 01's speed: a hammer striking it 18 times a second for 2.4 seconds, then the same short ring-out of about 0.8 s, ending in silence. It is the same clip the game builds when it loads, exported from the editor. To switch to a different bell, give its number.

Method

How they are made

A struck object, not a tone

  • Each bell is a set of resonating partials (modes) at inharmonic ratios, the way metal rings. A strike kicks every partial at once and each one then decays at its own rate: high ones die first.
  • Pairs of partials a few hertz apart give the slow shimmer of a real bell.
  • Each strike adds a short burst of bright noise, the click of the hammer or clapper on metal.

The electric bell

  • The hammer comes back 15 to 50 times a second (it depends on the bell). On each hit it touches the gong for a few milliseconds and damps it, then lets it ring again. That dip and the click are the "rrrring" rhythm.
  • Strike timing varies by ±2% and strength by up to 15%, from a fixed random seed, so it is never machine-perfect but always the same.

Made for a phone speaker

  • The energy sits mostly between about 500 Hz and 4 kHz. Nothing depends on bass a phone cannot play; the big bells' low hum is filtered out. The small gong (02 and 15) also has two faint partials at 4.9 and 6.2 kHz, about 7% of its energy, which add sparkle where the speaker can play them.
  • A few early reflections and a short room tail put the bell in a room, not in the menu.

Same loudness rules for all

  • Peak at most 0.30 of full scale (the limit is 0.35). Nothing clips.
  • No variant is louder than the in-game bell: each is turned down until its loudest 0.4 s is no louder than number 01 (0.0994), which is close to the old bell (0.0913). The bell in the game, 15, measures 0.0877.
  • Every clip fades to exactly zero with a raised-cosine curve, so it never ends on a click.

What the game's test checks for the bell in use

  • It can be heard within 30 ms of time running out (measured: 5 ms).
  • Its envelope repeats 15 to 25 times a second (measured: 18.2), so it rings in a rhythm and is not a steady tone.
  • It rings loudly for 2 to 3 seconds (measured: loud until 2.61 s, ring-out included) and the whole clip is 2.8 to 3.6 s (measured: 3.2 s).
  • Peak between 0.2 and 0.35 (measured: 0.30), and the last 20 ms are below 1% of the peak, ending on zero.
