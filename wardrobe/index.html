Wardrobe

The player's supervisor will get a wardrobe: hats, tops, trousers, shoes, badges and extras, each worn or taken off. Phase 1 gives the supervisor a real body. The stack of cubes is gone. The supervisor now uses the students' skinned body and skeleton, as a man or a woman (the player's choice, saved with the career). The supervisor walks with a real walk cycle, holds a hand out when acting on a student, wears the bought shirt colour, and wears the badge on the front of the chest. Phase 2 gives the wardrobe its eight slots and puts a Wardrobe in the store: a live 3D figure of your own supervisor, a Man / Woman toggle, category tabs, try-on before buying, and Buy → Wear → Take off on every item. Phase 3 is in review: 36 candidate items (hats, tops, trousers, shoes, pin badges and school crests, glasses, a lanyard and a watch), five full outfits, three hairstyles for the woman and the fix for the seams on her tops, for you to choose which go into the game. Still to come: bringing the chosen items in, then prices and unlocks (4).

In the room

Play mode on a phone (1080 × 2340) in two schools' rooms, with the Bronze badge on and the issued teal shirt. Standing beside the supervisor's desk when the room opens, and mid-walk a moment after a tap. When standing, the supervisor faces the camera, turned slightly toward the room's centre, so the face and the badge show.

The walk

20 frames a second of game time while the supervisor crosses the front of the room at full speed: first the man, then the woman. The left clip uses the game's camera angle, closer in. The right clip shows the supervisor side-on. The walk is the students' own walk clip from candidate.glb; no new clip was made. Its speed follows the supervisor's actual speed, so the planted foot stays put on the floor. The blend from standing to walking follows the speed too, so starting and stopping are gradual. A footstep sound plays each time a foot lands.

Walk speed lowered from 3.2 to 2.1 m/s (brisk walk), the user's choice. 3.2 m/s is a jog: a walk would have to play at 2.3× its speed and would look like scurrying. At 2.1 m/s the clip plays at 1.5×. The clip's planted foot travels 1.40 m/s at normal speed, measured from the clip. The balance bots now walk at 2.1 m/s too. Their twelve careful test careers were re-recorded: median final coins went from 1017 to 1010, median reputation from 70 to 68.5, and none was dismissed.

The supervisor, close up

The same figure the room uses, built on its own: as issued (teal shirt, no badge), and with the Navy Shirt and the Bronze Badge. The badge is a small disc on the chest bone, over the wearer's left chest, placed against the front of each body's shirt. It moves with the chest. Before, it was a cube that floated behind the supervisor's back.

Students are unchanged

The students' body file, parts file and prefab are untouched. The supervisor uses them read-only and has its own material. In the Maple room the same class is seated before and after the change: the students are identical, and only the supervisor differs. In the National room the class draw depends on the frame count, so different people are seated, but they are drawn the same way. A test builds both supervisors and checks that every student renderer's colour and material is unchanged.

Phase 2: the Wardrobe

The store's "Appearance" section is now the Wardrobe. Play mode at a phone's portrait size (1080 × 2340), dark theme, with the Navy Shirt and the Bronze Badge owned, the badge worn, and 160 coins.

Eight slots

Hat, top, trousers, shoes, badge, glasses, neck (lanyard or tie) and wrist (watch). One item each, owned items only. Every slot can be emptied except the top: taking a top off puts the issued shirt back on. Saves move from version 3 to 4; the old "shirt" becomes the top, so nothing worn or owned is lost.

The preview stage

The same figure as the room, on its own layer far below the room, filmed by its own camera into a 512 × 640 texture (about 2.6 MB). Its two lights are on only while that camera renders, and the room's lights are off for that moment, so neither lights the other. It renders only while the card is on screen and is destroyed, texture and all, when you leave the store.

Ready for art

Hats, glasses, ties and watches already have a place on the right bone (head, face, neck, left wrist). Phase 3 adds a catalogue line, a prefab named after the item and a shop tile; the tab appears by itself.

Never gameplay

Trying on never touches the save. Nothing in the simulation reads what is worn or which body was chosen (tested).

Phase 1's two loose ends are fixed. The room now frames the supervisor at the body's full width with its hands (0.45 m each side, not the 0.35 m footprint), so the whole figure is on screen at the start in every school on 19.5:9, 20:9 and 21:9 phones and 4:3 and 16:10 tablets, checked vertex by vertex through the real camera. In Maple on a phone the right hand is now at 92% of the width. And the body turns before it walks: from rest, it no longer walks sideways while the turn catches up.

Phase 3 — candidates · not in the game yet

Phase 3: the clothing, to choose from

36 candidate items, each worn by both the man and the woman, for you to pick which go into the game. They are Blender renders of the game's own meshes: the shipped body, skeleton and parts, in the Standing clip, at the store preview's size (512 × 640), with one fixed 3/4-front camera and one light set for every shot. Small items get a close-up too. Everything else is the issued look (teal top, dark trousers, dark shoes). No item here is in the game yet; after you choose, step B brings the chosen ones in.

How to read a card. Name, slot, a suggested price band and the technical route. Rigid: one mesh on one bone, like the badge today. Skinned: a new part cut from each body's own shape, so it bends with the arms like the shipped tops (one per body). Colour: a tint of something that already exists, so there is no new mesh at all.

The seams on the woman's tops: found and fixed

Before · shipped Top_LongSleeve_F

Thin light slits at the shoulder and at the sides of the waist.

After · the same cut, closed

No slits, same shape and weights.

Cause. To shape her bust, build_female.py subdivides the chest of the male body once. The faces inside that patch gain a vertex in the middle of each edge; the face across the patch's border keeps its plain edge. The body is flat-shaded, so the tops are cut from it and welded by position, and a vertex in the middle of an edge has nothing to weld to. The gap is invisible until the vertex moves (the bust) and the shell is pushed out along its normals. Then it opens into a slit. There are exactly 12 of these on each female top (short-sleeve, hoodie, long-sleeve) and none on the male tops. Fix. When the shell is cut, each such edge is split at the middle vertex and welded (fix_tjunctions, a few lines in build_parts.shell). It also fixes the female students, who wear the same parts. Every woman's top on this page already uses it.

Mesh count

7 colour-only items (3 T-shirts, the hoodie, 3 trousers), 5 new tops (×2 bodies), 2 shoe parts (×2 bodies), and 18 rigid meshes (5 hats, 2 glasses, a lanyard, a watch, 3 pins sharing one mesh, 8 crests sharing one shield).

Mobile cost

Every new piece is a few hundred triangles at most and uses the role materials the figure already tints. A second colour (tie, sole, cuff, emblem) is a second role on the same white material, not a texture.

Shop tiles

Each chosen item needs a tile through the shop tile pipeline (owned by another session); none were made here.

How it works

One builder

SupervisorFigure.Build(prefab, appearance, parent) makes a dressed figure anywhere, from a SupervisorAppearance (body, hair and every wardrobe slot). The room and the store's live preview both use it.

Man or woman

Saved in the career as SupervisorBody, Male by default. Older saves load as Male. It is only a look: the simulation never reads it. The Wardrobe's toggle changes it at any time, free.

Three clips, one mixer

Standing, Walking and PresentDocument (the reach), from the students' file. They are mixed by speed on a hand-driven graph, with no per-frame allocation.

Mobile cost

One skinned body, two or three skinned parts, a badge disc and one material.

Known limits

  • Shirt seams. The female tops (and the female students' tops) show a few thin seams in the game today. The cause is found and the fix is shown above; it reaches the game with phase 3's integration.
  • The clothing is not in the game yet. The phase 3 items above are candidates rendered in Blender from the game's meshes. Until the chosen ones are brought in, the store has only tops and badges.
