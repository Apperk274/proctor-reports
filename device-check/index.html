Phone Check

The game on a real Galaxy A15 (SM-S156V, Android 16, 1080 × 2340, 450 dpi, 90 Hz). The first pass, on main at bf08cc4, found one blocking bug and four smaller ones. They are fixed on branch m130-1-device-fixes (three commits on 881d1cf, not merged). That build was installed on the same phone and every case was checked again. The game still classifies the screen as a phone, and every room has the right number of desks with at least one free.

What the phone found, and what changed

Found on bf08cc4NowChange
Blocking: National front-row focus showed only greyfixed The front wall is a 2.4 m cutaway in every room. It was 5 m in the hall. The focus pose is now solved in Core (RoomFraming.FocusPosition). If the front wall would ever hide the student, the camera moves along its own line into the room, so the aim stays the same. A new test checks, for every school, both device classes and every seat, that the student is visible over the front wall from both the focus camera and the overview.
Medium: the supervisor started behind the front wall, at the screen edge, or inside the deskfixed The supervisor now starts beside their desk, inside the room and in the overview's frame. Before, the start was 1.5 m outside the front wall. The supervisor's desk is also part of the navmesh now, so nobody can stand inside it. EditMode and PlayMode tests check that the start is inside the room, clear of every desk, chair and wall, on the navmesh, above the HUD, and not behind the wall.
Minor: a gold medal on an audit penalty; a sealed document on "You are not well"fixed "Seal" now shows the sealed letter. The medal is only for something earned: a move up, a commendation, the national assignment. Illness has no picture, because no baked render fits it. The full event-to-picture table is below and is locked by a test.
Minor: white papers almost invisible on white desksfixed Brightpath has grey laminate, National blue-grey desks and Maple pale-blue tops. Paper-to-desk contrast is now at least 1.5 in every style, and a test enforces 1.4 or more.
Minor: "Monthly expenses −280" had no ¢fixed It now reads −280 ¢. Every money row goes through one formatter (GameText.CoinDelta).
Boot warning "no valid NavMesh"unchanged This is the known start-up ordering race, documented in PlayerMover.Start. Start re-places the agent and logs Navigation ready right after. It is harmless and was left alone.
Admit taps dropped 2.5–5 s apartby design The Admit and Reject buttons unlock only once the person on the card is standing at the desk (HudRouter, M34). A tap during their walk-in is ignored. Nothing was changed.

A further bug was found during the fixes. With the supervisor starting beside the desk, an existing test caught the player walking 2.2 m on to an empty chair after the student they were walking to had left the room. The old start, far away, hid this. The walk now stops when its target leaves. Balance bots keep the old start, because moving them changes every balance fingerprint. That is a balance decision, not a framing fix.

Notes

  • Logcat. In both passes, 20 launches in total, there were no crashes, no ANRs and no managed exceptions. The development build always logs profiler-socket messages, and every launch logs the NavMesh warning described above.
  • How the schools were reached. Local copies of the save were edited, setting State.Career.InstitutionId, or adding a scheduled offer event for the offer cards, and pushed to the phone. The user's own save was backed up and checksummed first, then restored at the end with matching checksums. No save file is published here.
  • Merge. Main gained three commits after the fix branch was cut (sound rows, Phone Detector, Pills). A trial merge is textually clean but has not been built or tested. Main's new Pills art is a drawn shop tile, which could later be the Illness picture.
  • Not checked. The day hero renders (there is no light theme in the game), a tablet (none attached), and offers produced by real play.
