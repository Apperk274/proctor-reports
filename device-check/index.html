Phone Check

Main at bf08cc4 (M129 schools and offers, the baked Blender art, M130 rooms), built as a development APK and played on a real Galaxy A15 (SM-S156V, Android 16, 1080 × 2340, 450 dpi, 90 Hz). The game classified the screen as a phone, and every school's room had the right number of desks, with at least one left free. Most of it works. One bug blocks play: at the National Examination Centre, leaning in on a front-row student leaves the screen blank.

Blocking: the camera goes blank on a front-row student at the National Examination Centre

Tap a front-row student in the National hall. The supervisor walks over and the camera leans in. It ends up outside the room, looking at the back of the front wall. Only the HUD and the action panel are left on a flat grey screen. It stays like that: Back does nothing, and with no room on screen the player cannot tap anyone else.

Likely cause: the focus pose in FixedCameraRig.FocusPosition() puts the camera about 5.4 m in front of the student (7 m at 46°, plus the screen-height bias). Front-row seats are 4 m from the front wall (RoomLayout.FrontClearance), so the camera lands about 1.4 m outside the room. The exam hall's front wall is 5.0 m tall (RoomDresser, ExamHall WallHeight = 5.0f), which is higher than the line from the camera to the student. The 3.0 m and 3.6 m walls in the other rooms are low enough. On the State University front row (3.6 m wall) the wall only covers the bottom of the frame, behind the action panel. The code has not been changed; the fix needs approval.

Other issues

  • Medium: the supervisor is often off screen when the exam starts. At Kingsley, City High and National the supervisor stands at the front wall and is hidden by it. Only the top of the head shows at City High (room-cityhigh, bottom centre), and at Maple the figure is cut by the screen edge. At Eastgate the supervisor is visible but stands inside the supervisor's desk. This is probably the same cause as the blocking bug: the room's front edge is very close to the camera's frame.
  • Minor: event pictures do not always fit the event. A medal for an admissions-audit penalty (event-audit), a sealed document for a headache (event-unwell).
  • Minor: exam papers on white desks. At Brightpath and National the papers are white on white and almost disappear.
  • Minor: the results card mixes currency formats. "Monthly expenses −280" has no ¢, while the lines above it do (results).
  • Minor: a NavMesh warning at boot. Every launch logs Failed to create agent because there is no valid NavMesh about 0.7 s before Navigation ready. Walking works, so this is a start-up ordering warning.
  • Note: admission taps are sometimes dropped. Taps on Admit sent 2.5–5 s apart were often ignored (8 taps admitted 4 at National). With one tap every 7 s, each tap worked. This may be a deliberate input lock while the student walks in, but a player tapping quickly will notice it.

Logcat

  • No crash, no ANR, no managed exception from the game in 10 launches.
  • Each launch: Unable to setup multicast socket for player connection / Socket: Failed to set blocking mode. These are the development build's profiler socket and are harmless.
  • Each launch: Failed to create agent because there is no valid NavMesh, then Navigation ready. Listed as a minor issue above.
  • Everything else in the log came from other apps (Google auth, Nearby, a React Native app).

How the schools were reached

  • There is no developer setting for the school (GameBootstrap only has a device-class override). Copies of the save were edited on the computer, setting State.Career.InstitutionId to each school, and pushed as the save. The offer cards came from copies with a scheduled PromotionOffer event added, so they show the real card for a made-up offer.
  • The user's own save (at Kingsley Academy) was copied off the phone and checksummed before installing. At the end, the same files were pushed back and the checksums matched. No save file is published here.
  • Not checked: the day hero renders (no light theme in game), a tablet (no tablet attached), the 5 redrawn icons one by one (they appear in the Career/Store/Manual tab bar but were not opened), and offers produced by real play.
