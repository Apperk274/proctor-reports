Texture Compression

The baked Blender renders (hub heroes, title, exam hall, event objects, shop tiles) ship as ASTC 6×6. This compares stronger settings: the APK size of a clean build for each, and the pixels the phone's GPU really decodes, side by side with today's.

114.63 MB

Today's development APK, from a clean build. Rebuilt three times at the shipped settings: the same size each time, within 8 bytes.

11.4 MB

The 72 baked renders in the build, out of 15.8 MB of textures and 33.6 MB of user assets.

−3.55 MB

The recommendation: scenes and event objects to ASTC 8×8, shop tiles left alone. No difference at phone size.

−6.14 MB

The most this measured: everything at 8×8 and the daytime renders left out, which needs a decision first.

What the phone draws

Each picture was imported with the setting shown, and the GPU decoded the real ASTC texture. The texture was then drawn at the size the phone shows it (renders are made at 1.25× that) and read back. "Source" is the same path with no compression. The first row of each subject is phone pixels 1:1. The second is a part of it at 2× with nearest-neighbour, so every pixel shows. The dB figure is the whole picture's PSNR against the source at phone size: higher is closer. On a laptop screen, 1:1 is already larger than on a phone, whose pixels are about three times smaller.

Gradients at six times the contrast

Banding shows first in smooth skies and dark glows, so these smooth areas are stretched to six times their contrast around the source's average colour. At normal contrast every column here looks the same. This is the stress test, not what a player sees.

Where quality suffers

  • 8×8, at phone size: no visible difference. In every 1:1 crop (dusk and day skies, the title room, the dark note and doorway, the tiles), 8×8 can't be told from 6×6.
  • 8×8, at 2×: slightly softer hard edges. The moon's rim, the dome's finial and the paper edges are a touch less crisp, and some stars lose a little brightness. No blocks show.
  • 8×8, at 6× contrast: faint bands in the dusk sky. 6×6 shows them too, more weakly. The day sky and the title room's wall stay clean.
  • 10×10 breaks first on the small art. At 2×, the signal detector's edges and the blazer's lapels go visibly blocky, and at 1:1 the detector's green bars look smeared. Avoid it.
  • Max size 1024 is the wrong lever. Shrinking to 1024 and then drawing at about 998 px resamples the picture twice. Columns, the dome and the desks go soft at 1:1, the PSNR falls below 8×8 at full size, and it saves less (−2.34 MB against −2.49 MB for heroes at 8×8). At 8×8 and 1024, the dusk sky bands are visible at 6× contrast.

Recommendation

  • Now: ASTC 8×8 for every scene and event object, shop tiles unchanged. −3.55 MB of APK (114.63 → 111.08 MB), and the art in the build drops from 11.4 to 7.3 MB, also in GPU memory while it is on screen. On the branch, this is the proposal commit e0d4156. It changes one line in BakedArtImporter (tiles stay at 6×6, everything else goes to 8×8) and the Android/iOS format in 28 .meta files. Its build came out at exactly the measured size.
  • Then: the shop tiles, if their owner agrees. Taking the store and wardrobe tiles to 8×8 too adds about 0.9 MB (all-8×8 is 110.20 MB). 0.68 MB of that is the 35 wardrobe tiles. On the 2× tile crops, 8×8 is only slightly softer.
  • Decide on the daytime renders. If no light theme is planned soon, leaving them out saves another 1.7 MB at 8×8 (108.50 MB in all). Scenery already falls back to the dusk render when a day one is missing.
  • Not 10×10 and not a smaller max size. See above.

Outside the baked art

  • candidate.glb is the biggest single asset. It is a 3.2 MB file that becomes 10.6 MB in the build, mostly animation (10.4 MB in all). It comes in through glTFast, which brings the baked keyframes in as they are. Lowering the bake rate at export, or reducing the keyframes, would very likely save more than any texture setting here. It belongs to the candidate and wardrobe work, so it was not touched or measured.
  • The remaining 4.4 MB of textures are mostly URP's own (SMAA, film grain) and the characters'. Nothing obvious and cheap there.

How this was measured

  • Builds. Batchmode, Unity 6000.3.22f1, BuildAndroid.Development, one worktree of main eed4d45. The option comes from an environment variable read by TextureCompressionOptions.cs on the branch. Without it, the shipped settings apply.
  • A clean APK takes more than deleting Builds/Android. Unity copies the APK out of Gradle's output in Library/Bee/…/launcher/build/outputs/apk, and Gradle repacks that file incrementally, so a smaller build kept the old file's length. The first round of sizes was wrong for this reason. Every size here comes from a build with Gradle's packaging output deleted, and the sum of the zip entries agrees with it.
  • Pixels. CaptureQuality imports copies of the renders at each format, draws the GPU texture (ASTC, decoded by the GPU) to a render target, and reads it back. Read back at full size, the uncompressed path matches the source PNG exactly, so the colour handling adds no error of its own.
  • To re-run: Tools/texcomp/run_all.sh, then crops.py, metrics.py and buildreport.py on the results.
