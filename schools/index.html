Schools and Offers

A career now runs through eight schools instead of three postings. Each school has a fixed star rating that the player can see. A move between schools arrives as an offer, and the offer compares the school you hold with the one offered, on both rating and salary. Usually one school writes. Sometimes two write at once, and then a better-rated school competes with a lower-rated one that pays more. The screenshots are from Play mode at a phone's portrait size (1080 × 2340).

The schools

A career starts at Maple at standing 40. "Offered at" is the standing a school asks for. "Lost below" is the standing under which you are reassigned. The two numbers are kept apart so that a career does not bounce between schools. Brightpath, highlighted, is the lower-rated school that pays more.

At Brightpath a good exam earns 1 standing instead of 2, so the extra money costs a slower climb. Every school that pays more than a better-rated one has to carry a cost like this, and a test checks that.

The offer card

Each school offered gets its own panel. It shows the rating as stars now › stars offered, and the salary as now › offered with the change. The buttons name the school.

Two schools at once

  • Two offers arrive on one card, as one decision. The player takes one school or declines both, so taking one can never leave the other open.
  • The second school is rated lower and pays more. It appears beside a promotion only when such a school is open to the player: City High (better rated) against Brightpath (better paid).
  • How often: about one card in fourteen in a careful career.
  • The cost of the money is printed on the card, not hidden in the rules.
  • Without a banner. The comparison stands in for the picture, so the three answers fit on the screen.

A worse school that pays more

  • When no promotion is due, a school rated no higher may ask on its own for more money. Here the rating goes down from three stars to two, and the salary goes up by 40.
  • Rarer than a promotion: a 25% chance, with at least six exams between offers.
  • In 22 of 24 careful careers, the better-paid, lower-rated option appeared at least once.

Where you work

The hub shows the school's stars and kind under its name. The building follows the kind of school: schools are drawn as a school, the evening college as the professional building, and the academy, university and examination centre as the colonnade.

Open points
